## 2026-07-30 - Insecure Direct Object Reference (IDOR) on Company Users' Scoped Resources
**Vulnerability:** Users with the `company_user` role could potentially access, view, or modify details of other companies' clients, financings, or transactions by supplying their database IDs. The corresponding Laravel Policy methods only performed generic permission-level checks (`$user->can('view_financing')`) instead of validating ownership / model-level association (`$user->company_id === $model->company_id`).
**Learning:** Overriding `getEloquentQuery()` in Filament resources only scopes query lists. It does not automatically prevent direct URL access to editing/viewing/deleting specific records if the associated Model Policies lack model-level ownership checks.
**Prevention:** Always ensure Model Policies validate model-level relationships or tenant associations (e.g., matching `company_id` for scoped user roles) on action-specific methods (`view`, `update`, `delete`, etc.), rather than relying on global list-level filtering or general permission-wide checks.
